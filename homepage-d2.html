For the doers, problem solvers, and those who get things done, welcome to The Policy Circle, a vibrant community of over 15,000 women in 48 states who care deeply about preserving freedom, free markets, personal responsibility, and entrepreneurial values.

We firmly believe that despite significant progress in recent decades, far too few women hold seats of influence in their communities. We’re dedicated to changing this by equipping and empowering women to step into leadership roles.

We gather women together who want to bring back civil discourse to discuss pressing public policy issues while asking each other tough questions, diving into meaningful conversations focused on solutions at the local level. Since 2015, The Policy Circle has been on a mission to build up women as strong, focused, and effective leaders.

BUILDING WOMEN LEADERS

The Policy Circle offers Civic Leadership Engagement Roadmap (CLER), a three-month leadership program that boosts civic knowledge and propels women into leadership. Launched in 2021, this highly immersive, experiential program combines small group learning and one-onone coaching to equip graduates with the constitutional knowledge, network, and support to lead. Over 300 participants have enrolled in the program that launched them as community leaders, securing both elected and appointed roles.
